A trio that grew out of jam sessions in early 2014 — clawhammer banjo, double bass, drums; the songbook drawn heavily from Leadbelly. Documented gigs at Lefty's Old Time Music Hall in Brisbane and the Northern Rivers Hotel in North Lismore. (Worth confirming the spelling — *Healers* per Banjo Hangout, but *Heelers* would be the cattle-dog joke.)
