Three decades and counting of "Funkafide Blues" — Miss Jodie out front with that big upfront voice, a chunky rhythm section, a four-piece horn line on a good night, and Julian's congas and flute weaving through the back end. The thing that sets the Hoochers apart is the same thing that makes them hard to slot into a single genre: they grew out of Sunshine Coast roots and have been folding in funk, swing and jive ever since.
