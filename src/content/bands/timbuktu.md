A Northern Rivers Afrobeat / Highlife / Ethio-jazz ensemble built around Joe O'Keeffe's looped guitar — Cam Smith and Dave Stephenson on brass, Brent Calcutt's keys holding the harmonic centre, Anthony Mitchell's bass and Benji Keogh's drums driving it, Rod Evans on percussion, and Julian on flute weaving across the top.
