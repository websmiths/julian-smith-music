Sofiella Watt and Huckleberry Jim formed their alliance during time on a remote Pacific island, then toured Canada as The Huckleberry Bandits — picking up a Kootenay Music Award for Best Folk/Country/Roots Act along the way. Now based in the Byron Shire, the five-piece weaves banjo, washboard, viola, guitars, drums and double bass into something the band describes as "winsome folk, bruised with blues."
