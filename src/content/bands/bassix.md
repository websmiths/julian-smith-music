Bassix is a vehicle for Miss Jodie's songs — comparisons to Janis Joplin are not unwarranted — with Julian's upright bass holding the floor and the sound desk both. The band scales: from a duo at the smallest end up to a five-piece with horns. The *Live at the Duck Pond* set was recorded in South Lismore.
