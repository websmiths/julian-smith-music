A rotating folk collective that plays "as if around a big old kitchen table" — sweet harmony, old-time and bluegrass instrumentation, and a self-description that earns its space: "slowly-decolonising-coloniser folk fanfare." Proceeds go to the Sierra Learning Centre in Freetown.
