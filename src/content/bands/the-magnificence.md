The Magnificence formed in 2017 around a love of New Orleans jazz, gospel touches and originals that wear their influences without pretending to be anything other than what they are. Recorded between the Northern Rivers Conservatorium in Lismore and The Barn Studio in Plainland, Queensland — three releases and counting, with airplay reaching as far as WWOZ FM in New Orleans.
